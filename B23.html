Reader Questions, Answered Honestly

The best guides we've written started as customer questions. "Can I use a pressure washer instead of hydro jetting?" (No — wrong pressure profile, wrong nozzles, and you'll flood your own basement.) "Will a water softener help my drains?" (It helps with scale in supply lines, not with grease in drain lines — different systems.) "How do I find my sewer cleanout?" (Look for a capped pipe near the foundation, in the yard along the line to the street, or in the garage; if you can't find it, we'll locate it with the camera on your first visit.) "Is trenchless repair as strong as new pipe?" (The liner is independently structural — it's a new pipe inside the old one, rated for decades.)
